Learned Dense Front-End

Tightly-Coupled Visual-Inertial SLAM with a Learned Dense Front-End

M.Sc. Thesis, TUM In Progress
SLAM Visual-Inertial Odometry Factor Graphs 3D Foundation Models

Modern learned SLAM systems (VGGT-SLAM, MASt3R-SLAM, ViSTA) regress dense 3D pointmaps directly from images, producing excellent scene geometry, but being monocular, they cannot recover metric scale and their trajectories drift. This thesis fuses a learned pointmap front-end with an IMU inside a single probabilistic factor graph, using learned uncertainty to weight the visual geometry while the IMU pins down absolute scale and gravity. The core contribution is the coupling itself: defining the visual measurement, weighting it, assigning ownership of scale, and closing loops from the network's own predictions, toward a real-time system with metric, locally-consistent trajectories.
